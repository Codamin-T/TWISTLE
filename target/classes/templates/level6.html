6 letters

This is the six letter game. After each guess, the letters will turn different colors. Green means that the letter is in the correct spot. Yellow means that the letter exists in the word, but is in the incorrect spot. Grey means that the letter does not exist in the word.

How to Play

Guess the hidden word before you run out of tries!


🟢 Green — correct letter, correct spot

🟡 Yellow — correct letter, wrong spot

⚫ Grey — letter not in the word
